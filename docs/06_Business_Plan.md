# SkillRoute — Business Plan

## Company overview
- Industry: EdTech / Career Readiness / Student Productivity
- Proposed base: Goa, India
- Company type: technology startup; private limited structure proposed after validation
- Objective: help students move from career uncertainty to clear, measurable next actions.

## Problem
Students can have a career goal without a clear understanding of which skills, tools and practical experience they should prioritize. Information exists across role descriptions, learning platforms, roadmaps, communities and spreadsheets, but is not organized around the individual student.

## Solution
**Career Goal → Student Profile → Skill Assessment → Skill Gap Analysis → Personalized Roadmap → Progress → Reassessment → Updated Roadmap**

SkillRoute is a decision-and-planning layer, not another course catalogue.

## Market
The Ministry of Education's AISHE 2021-22 report estimates higher-education enrolment in India at **43,268,181 students**. This is a broad addressable baseline, not a claim that every enrolled student is a customer.

Illustrative sizing:
- TAM: 43.27M students (AISHE baseline)
- SAM: 20% assumed relevant to technology/professional career planning = 8.65M
- Year-3 SOM: 4,500 Pro users + 25 campuses × 800 students = 24,500 student relationships

## Competition
| Alternative | Strength | SkillRoute opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Learn | Career paths, plans and role-oriented learning | Profile → gap → next-action loop |
| Coursera | Large catalogue, projects and subscriptions | Planning and prioritization layer |
| Generic roadmaps/search | Abundant and often free | Personalization + reassessment |

## Advantage
Personal profile + role requirements + prioritized gaps + linked projects + reassessment.

## GTM
0–3 months: 1–2 career roles, 3 pilot campuses, 300–500 testers.

3–6 months: campus/community growth, content and referral loops.

6–12 months: Pro launch, conversion and retention measurement.

Year 2+: more roles, institutional analytics and portfolio/project features.

## Risks
- Generic recommendations → grounded role/skill mappings and “why this matters” explanations.
- Low retention → useful progress + reassessment and one clear next action.
- High CAC → campus, community and referral channels before paid scale.
- AI errors → constrained datasets + evaluation examples.
- Privacy → minimum necessary data; synthetic/anonymized prototype data.
