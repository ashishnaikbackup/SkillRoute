# SkillRoute — Business Model

**Tagline:** Know what to learn. Know what's next.

## Customer segments
- Primary: college and university students exploring technology or professional careers.
- Secondary buyers: colleges, placement cells, training teams and student communities.
- Beachhead: internship- and placement-focused students.

## Value proposition
For college students who know the career direction they want but are unsure which skills to prioritize, SkillRoute is a career skill-planning platform that turns their current profile and career goal into a personalized, actionable roadmap. Unlike generic course browsing or static roadmaps, SkillRoute identifies skill gaps, prioritizes next steps and updates the plan as the student progresses.

## Revenue model
| Stream | Offer | Proposed pricing |
|---|---|---:|
| Free | Goal + profile + roadmap preview | ₹0 |
| Pro | AI gap analysis, roadmap, projects, reassessment | ₹149/month |
| Campus | Institution dashboard + cohort insights | ₹60/student/year |
| Career Packs | Focused route bundles | ₹499/sale |

## Go-to-market
1. Campus partnerships with placement cells and student clubs.
2. Content marketing around “what should I learn next?”.
3. Referral loops through route sharing and progress milestones.
4. Creator partnerships after organic traction.
5. Paid acquisition only after CAC and activation are understood.

## Key partners
Colleges, student clubs, learning/content providers, and AI/cloud infrastructure vendors.

## Key resources
Role-skill dataset, product engineering, UX, AI evaluation layer, cloud infrastructure, and student acquisition channels.

## Key activities
Maintain role-skill mappings, generate and evaluate routes, improve onboarding/reassessment, acquire/support students, and measure activation, retention and conversion.
